Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version: You can use Litly without an account. Posts disappear for everyone every day. We don't sell your data and we don't run ads. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we store your name and email, and we never show them on your posts.

What we collect

  • Posts, replies and hearts. What you write, the avatar that goes with it, and when you wrote it.
  • A scrambled version of your IP address. We turn your IP address into a one-way hash using a secret key, and we don't store the raw address. We use the hash to limit spam, count one heart per person, and block abusers.
  • If you sign in: your name, email address and profile picture URL from Google or Apple, plus a session so you stay signed in. If you use "Hide My Email" on Apple, we only get Apple's relay address.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider, such as request time, browser type and approximate location, used to keep the service secure and running.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't show your name or email on anything you post. Posting stays anonymous, whether or not you have an account.
  • We don't connect your posts to your account.

How long we keep things

  • Posts, replies and hearts are removed for everyone at the same time every day (currently 04:00 UTC). Deletion is automatic and runs shortly after the reset. Backups made by our database provider may keep older data for a limited time, up to about 30 days, before it is permanently erased.
  • Account details stay until you ask us to delete your account.
  • IP hashes are kept only as long as needed for spam limits and abuse prevention.

Cookies and local storage

We use only what's needed to run Litly, with no advertising or tracking cookies:

  • A random ID stored in your browser (local storage) that generates your daily avatar and remembers your shuffle count. It's not linked to your identity.
  • A sign-in cookie, only if you sign in, to keep you signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • A short-lived security cookie while you sign in with Google or Apple.

Who we share data with

We use a few services to run Litly: Cloudflare (hosting and database) and, if you choose to sign in, Google or Apple. They process data only to provide those services. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect people's safety and the service from abuse.

Your choices

  • Use Litly without an account. You never have to sign in to read or post.
  • To access, correct or delete your account information, email us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
  • Because posts aren't tied to accounts, we usually can't tell which posts are yours. Posts clear every day anyway.
  • To remove Litly's access to your Google or Apple login, remove Litly in your Google or Apple account settings.

Safety and moderation

We may review posts, remove content, and block people who break our rules or put others at risk. Litly is not a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, please contact local emergency services or a crisis line right away.

Children

Litly is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has used Litly, contact us and we'll remove their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the changes are significant, we'll post a notice on the site.
